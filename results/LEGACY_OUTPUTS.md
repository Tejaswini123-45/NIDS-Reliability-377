# Legacy outputs

These files predate the paper 377 revision audit and are retained for
traceability. The CICIDS2017 exports lack complete prediction and per-seed
provenance. The historical UNSW-NB15 outputs also use the earlier holdout
naming and FPR definition. Use the new separately generated
`verified_runs/` results, the standalone prediction verifier, and the
evidence package status before quoting metrics.
